
PI0COIN QSCI SYSTEM MANAGEMENT & RESOURCE ALLOCATION FRAMEWORK
===========================================================

1. RESOURCE ALLOCATION OPERATORS
------------------------------
Operator: processor_capacity_limit
Function: Maintains processing within 33% threshold
Equation: available_processing = total_processor_capacity * 0.33

Operator: storage_capacity_limit
Function: Maintains storage within 11% threshold
Equation: available_storage = total_storage_capacity * 0.11

Operator: qsci_float_maintenance
Function: Maintains 10,000,000 Pi0Coin float
Equation: required_minting = 10000000 - current_float

2. STORAGE ALLOCATION FORMULAS
----------------------------
Hot Storage Calculation:
$$ hot\_storage = min(daily\_requirement * 30, storage\_capacity\_limit * 0.4) $$

Warm Storage Calculation:
$$ warm\_storage = min(daily\_requirement * 365, storage\_capacity\_limit * 0.3) $$

Cold Storage Calculation:
$$ cold\_storage = min(daily\_requirement * 1825, storage\_capacity\_limit * 0.3) $$

Heat Sink Balance:
$$ heat\_sink\_allocation = hot\_storage * 0.15 + warm\_storage * 0.10 + cold\_storage * 0.05 $$

3. PROCESSING MANAGEMENT
----------------------
Daily Processing Allocation:
$$ daily\_processing = min(required\_processing, processor\_capacity\_limit) $$

QSci Processing Distribution:
$$ qsci\_processing = egin{cases}
    33\% & 	ext{for maintenance} \
    33\% & 	ext{for minting} \
    34\% & 	ext{for transactions}
\end{cases} $$

4. MINTING OPERATORS
------------------
Daily Minting Calculation:
$$ daily\_minting = max(0, required\_minting - current\_float) $$

Excess Harvesting:
$$ harvest\_amount = max(0, current\_float - 10000000) $$

5. RESOURCE MONITORING
--------------------
Processing Usage Tracking:
$$ processing\_usage = rac{active\_processes}{processor\_capacity\_limit} * 100 $$

Storage Usage Tracking:
$$ storage\_usage = rac{active\_storage}{storage\_capacity\_limit} * 100 $$

6. ANONYMITY PRESERVATION
-----------------------
Public Statistics Format:
- Total Processing Usage (%)
- Storage Distribution (Hot/Warm/Cold %)
- Aggregate Resource Utilization
- No user-specific data

7. MAINTENANCE AUTOMATION
-----------------------
Automated Maintenance Schedule:
$$ maintenance\_interval = egin{cases}
    hourly & 	ext{for critical systems} \
    daily & 	ext{for routine checks} \
    weekly & 	ext{for optimization} \
    monthly & 	ext{for deep maintenance}
\end{cases} $$

8. QSCI FLOAT MANAGEMENT
----------------------
Float Maintenance Formula:
$$ float\_adjustment = egin{cases}
    mint\_new & 	ext{if float < 10000000} \
    harvest\_excess & 	ext{if float > 10000000} \
    no\_action & 	ext{if float = 10000000}
\end{cases} $$

9. DYNAMIC CAPACITY MANAGEMENT
---------------------------
Storage Reallocation:
$$ storage\_reallocation = egin{cases}
    increase\_hot & 	ext{if hot\_usage > 90%} \
    increase\_warm & 	ext{if warm\_usage > 90%} \
    increase\_cold & 	ext{if cold\_usage > 90%}
\end{cases} $$

Processing Reallocation:
$$ processing\_reallocation = egin{cases}
    increase & 	ext{if usage > 90%} \
    decrease & 	ext{if usage < 10%} \
    maintain & 	ext{otherwise}
\end{cases} $$

10. SYSTEM CONSTRAINTS
-------------------
Processing Constraint:
$$ total\_processing \leq 33\% 	ext{ of total capacity} $$

Storage Constraint:
$$ total\_storage \leq 11\% 	ext{ of total capacity} $$

Data Retention:
$$ retention\_period \leq 5 	ext{ years} $$

11. OPERATIONAL EQUATIONS
-----------------------
Daily Requirements Calculation:
$$ daily\_req = rac{total\_annual\_requirement}{365} $$

Storage Distribution:
$$ storage\_distribution = egin{cases}
    40\% & 	ext{hot storage} \

\end{cases} $$

12. PERFORMANCE METRICS
--------------------
System Efficiency:
$$ efficiency = rac{actual\_throughput}{theoretical\_maximum} * 100 $$

Resource Utilization:
$$ utilization = rac{used\_resources}{allocated\_resources} * 100 $$

15. SYSTEM BALANCE
----------------
Heat Management:
$$ heat\_distribution = hot\_storage * 0.5 + warm\_storage * 0.3 + cold\_storage * 0.2 $$

$$ balance\_factor = rac{processing\_usage + storage\_usage}{2} $$
