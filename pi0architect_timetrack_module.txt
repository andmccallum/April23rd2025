Time Scale Definitions
--------------------
1. Planck Time Rate:
$$ PTR = BDR \cdot rac{t_p}{86400} $$
Where:
- BDR = Base Day Rate ($13,508.07)
- t_p = Planck time (5.391×10⁻⁴⁴ seconds)

2. Second Rate:
$$ SR(t) = rac{BDR}{86400} \cdot (1 + \sum_{i=1}^{n} F_i(t)) $$
Where:
- F_i = Temporal fluctuation factors

3. Fractal Time Scaling:
$$ FTS(t) = BDR \cdot \prod_{i=1}^{n} (rac{t_i}{t_{i-1}})^{D_i} $$
Where:
- t_i = Time scale levels
- D_i = Fractal dimension

TimeTrack Core Functions
----------------------
1. Task Time Capture:
$$ TTC(task) = \int_{t_start}^{t_end} SR(t) dt $$

2. Historical Time Integration:
$$ HTI = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \int_{t_i}^{t_{i+1}} TTC_i(t) dt $$

3. Blockchain Time Ledger:
$$ BTL = Hash(TTC + HTI + timestamp + Pi0Id) $$

4. Rate Adaptation Function:
$$ RAF(t) = SR(t) \cdot e^{\lambda \cdot t} \cdot \prod_{i=1}^{n} F_i $$
Where:
- λ = Learning rate
- F_i = Adaptation factors

System Operators
--------------
1. TimeScaleManager:
   Purpose: Manage time scales from Planck to Days
   $$ TSM(t) = \{PTR(t), SR(t), FTS(t)\} $$

2. TaskTracker:
   Purpose: Track and record task durations
   $$ TT(task) = \{start, end, duration, rate\} $$

3. HistoricalIntegrator:
   Purpose: Process and integrate past time records
   $$ HI(t) = \sum_{i=1}^{n} TT_i $$

4. BlockchainRecorder:
   Purpose: Record time entries in blockchain
   $$ BR(entry) = Hash(entry + timestamp) $$

5. RateAdjuster:
   Purpose: Dynamically adjust rates based on context
   $$ RA(t) = RAF(t) \cdot context_factor $$
